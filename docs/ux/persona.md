# Persona — "Grip"

Stage 2 (UI/UX) discovery output. The primary user is the product owner; this persona is grounded in the interview answers.

## Persona: The Building Beginner

**Name:** Omar (placeholder — the app owner)
**Age:** 20s
**Experience:** Beginner gym-goer (< 1 year)
**Goal:** Build muscle and maintain a healthy lifestyle.
**Attitude:** Motivated, wants to feel strong and in control, but still learning the lifts. Not intimidated by the gym — wants the app to feel exciting and empowering, not clinical.
**Frustrations (from Hevy):** Existing apps "lack many things" — too rigid, missing the workflow he wants, not built the way he trains. He's building his own because the tools don't fit.

## Context of use (critical for design)

- Uses the phone **one-handed** in the gym (other hand on the bar / equipment).
- Phone may be set down between sets; **sweaty/gloved fingers** tap the screen.
- Screen is used in **dim gym lighting** and near glare — needs high contrast.
- Sessions are short, focused bursts: log a set, rest, log again. No time for fiddly UI.
- Uses the **Start Workout screen every visit** — it is the most important surface in the product.

## Emotional direction (from interview)

1. **Energy & motivation** — the app should make training feel exciting; bold, athletic, alive.
2. **Quiet & numerical** — no confetti, no decoration around the data. Numbers are honest and clean. Energy comes from visuals and type, not gamification noise.

**Brand tension to protect:** *Loud visuals, quiet data.*

## Brand: "Grip"

Two meanings, both used:
- **The physical grip** — grabbing the bar, grip strength, the act of lifting.
- **Getting a grip on your fitness** — control, habit, structure, taking ownership.

**Tone of voice:** short, direct, confident. Commands ("FINISH SET", "END WORKOUT") over explanations.

## Design implications

- Dark, near-black UI with a single **volt lime** accent — high contrast, looks great in dim gyms.
- **Loud & Heavy** typography (Archivo Black) for names, numbers, and buttons.
- Large 48px+ touch targets, thumb-zone reachability, tabular figures so numbers never jitter.
- Hero screen: Start Workout with **List mode** and **Focus mode** + total timer.
- Feedback: numerical and immediate (volume ticks up), never cartoonish.
